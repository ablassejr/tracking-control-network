Seven structured worksheets - one per metalearning step from the background knowledge map. Each contains questions that require you to demonstrate understanding by **explaining in your own words**, **working through examples by hand**, and **connecting concepts to the paper**.

## How to Use

1. **Work through each step in order** (1 -> 7). Earlier steps build foundations for later ones.
2. **Write answers in the code blocks** provided under each question.
3. **Be honest with the self-assessment checklists** - unchecked items are study targets.
4. **Use the "Notes / Questions" sections** to capture anything unclear for your next meeting with Prof. Tymoshchuk.
5. **Revisit as the semester progresses** - your understanding will deepen.
