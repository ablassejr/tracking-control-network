The paper shows the controller works correctly if nonlinearity disturbances are bounded:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Signum disturbance: $0 \leq \rho < 1$
  \item Function $h$ disturbance: bounded by $\sqrt{f^2 + R^{-1}Q\,g^2}$
