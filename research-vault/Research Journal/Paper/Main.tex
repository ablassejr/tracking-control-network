\section{Abstract}
The simulation of a modified tracking control neural network for known affine
discrete-time nonlinear systems\textsubscript{\cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}}
will be ported to C++ and optimized. A previous simulation\textsubscript{\cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}}
of this system was designed in matlab. C++ gives lower level control to the
programmer, allowing for more granular and programmatic optimizations to be performed.
The modification to the tracking control network will to simulate adversarial attacks.
A modified system equation that seeks to minimize the impact of this style of a
dversarial attack will be designed and simulated.
