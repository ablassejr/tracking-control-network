\section{Abstract}
This paper will investigate the simulation of an adversarial attack on the
tracking control neural network for known affine discrete-time nonlinear
system given by \cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}. Additionally, the simulation
will be ported to C++ and optimized. The previous simulation \textsubscript{\cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}},
of the system was designed in MATLAB. This paper proposes that C++ is the
more optimal language for these simulations. C++ gives greater control of
the hardware resources involved in the computation to the programmer, allowing
for more granular optimizations to be performed.
