\subsubsection*{\centering Closed-Loop Tracking Control}
Substituting $u(k)$ from~\autoref{eq:control_function} into the
system model~\autoref{systemModel} gives the deployed closed-loop
state equation:
\begin{definition}
	Closed-Loop Tracking Control State Equation \textsubscript{\cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}}
	\begin{equation}
		\label{eq:closed_loop}
		\begin{aligned}
			 & x(k+1) = \state - \beta \text{sgn}(x(k) - r(k)) + \Delta r(k) \\
			 & \text{where:}                                                   \\
			 & \Delta r(k) = r(k+1) - r(k)
		\end{aligned}
	\end{equation}
\end{definition}
