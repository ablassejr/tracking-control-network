The difference equation \eqref{systemModel} and control equation \eqref{eq:control_function}
describe the full closed-loop dynamics. Substituting $u(k)$ from \eqref{eq:control_function}
into \eqref{systemModel} gives the closed-loop state equation~\cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}
\begin{equation}
	\label{eq:closed_loop}
	x(k+1) = x(k) - \beta \operatorname{sgn}(x(k) - r(k)) + \Delta r(k),
\end{equation}
where $\Delta r(k) = r(k+1) - r(k)$.
