\subsubsection*{\centering Closed-Loop Tracking Control}
The difference equation \autoref{systemModel} and control equation \autoref{eq:control_function}
describe the full closed-loop dynamics. Substituting $u(k)$ from \autoref{eq:control_function}
into \autoref{systemModel} gives the closed-loop state equation \textsubscript{\cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}}:
\begin{equation}
	\label{eq:closed_loop}
	\begin{aligned}
		 & x(k+1) = \state - \beta \text{sgn}(x(k) - r(k)) + \Delta r(k) \\
		 & \text{where:}                                                   \\
		 & \Delta r(k) = r(k+1) - r(k)
	\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
