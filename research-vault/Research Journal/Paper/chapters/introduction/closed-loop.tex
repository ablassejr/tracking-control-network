\subsubsection*{\centering Closed-Loop Tracking Control} % TODO: fix the version of the equation
Substituting $u(k)$ from~\autoref{eq:control_function} into the
system model~\autoref{systemModel} gives the deployed closed-loop
state equation:
\begin{definition}
	Closed-Loop Tracking Control State Equation \textsubscript{\cite{tymoshchuk2024tracking}}
	\begin{equation}
		x(k+1) = \state + \tau \beta \text{sgn}(x(k) - r(k)) + \Delta r(k)
	\end{equation}
	where:
	\begin{equation}
		\Delta r(k) = r(k+1) - r(k)
	\end{equation}
\end{definition}
