\begin{equation}
	\label{eq:error_dynamics}
	e(k+1) = e(k) - \beta \operatorname{sgn}(e(k)),
\end{equation}
\noindent where
\begin{equation*}
	\operatorname{sgn}(e(k)) = \begin{cases}
		1  & \text{if } e(k) > 0  \\
		0  & \text{if } e(k) = 0  \\
		-1 & \text{if } e(k) < 0
	\end{cases}
\end{equation*}
is the signum activation function~\cite{cichocki1993neural} of tracking error
$e(k) = x(k) - r(k)$. Notice that the difference equation
\eqref{eq:error_dynamics} includes the signum activation function and describes
supervised learning with learning rate parameter $\beta$ that can be used for
changing the convergence rate of the state variable trajectory $x(k)$ to the
reference trajectory $r(k)$. Since the activation function and supervised
learning are the basic characteristics of artificial
NNs~\cite{cichocki1993neural}, we treat the discrete-time tracking controller
described by the tracking equation \eqref{eq:closed_loop} and control equation
\eqref{eq:control_function} as a tracking control NN.
