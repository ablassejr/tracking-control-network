\par
The functional block-diagram of the defended system described by
\eqref{eq:noisy_sgn}--\eqref{eq:defended_closed_loop} is presented
in Fig.~\ref{fig:defended-system}. The tracking error~$e(k)$ enters
the signal function block~$\operatorname{sgn}$, whose output is
subject to the additive stochastic perturbation~$\epsilon(k)$,
producing the noisy signal~$\widetilde{s}(k)$
from~\eqref{eq:noisy_sgn}. The noisy signal is rounded to the
defended value~$\hat{s}(k)$ according
to~\eqref{eq:rounding_defense}. The defended output is then passed
to the amplification block~$\beta$. The amplified signal, together
with the reference increment~$\Delta r$ and the state-dependent
drift~$f(x(k))$, enters the LAESS block, which combines these
with~$g(x(k))$ to compute the defended control
input~$\hat{u}(k)$.
