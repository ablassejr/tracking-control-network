\subsection{Simulation of Defended CSTR System}

The voting scheme defense is applied to the CSTR system from
\autoref{eq:attacked_cstr_dynamics} under the same adversarial perturbation
($\sigma = 1$) used in \autoref{fig:attacked-state-tracking}. Three redundant
channels measure the reference signal; channel~3 is corrupted by additive
Gaussian noise on the reference path, while channels~1 and~2 receive the
reference exactly ($\delta_i = 0$). The minimum-variance pair selection
(\autoref{eq:pair_selection}) identifies and excludes the corrupted channel at
each time step, and the defended error $\hat{e}(k)$ from
\autoref{eq:defended_error} is used in the control law.

\autoref{fig:defended-state-tracking} presents the defended state trajectories
alongside the attacked trajectories from \autoref{fig:attacked-state-tracking}.
Under the voting scheme, the state variables $x_1(t)$ and $x_2(t)$ recover
nominal tracking performance, confirming the theoretical result of
\autoref{thm:defended_convergence} under ideal sensor conditions.
