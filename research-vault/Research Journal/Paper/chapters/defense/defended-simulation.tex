\subsection{Simulation of Defended System}

The rounding defense~\eqref{eq:rounding_defense} is applied to the CSTR
system from \eqref{eq:cstr_dynamics} using the same rejection-sampled
disturbance as the attacked simulation, so $|\epsilon(k)| < \tfrac{1}{2}$
holds by construction at every time step. The sufficient condition of the
rounding defense is therefore satisfied at every $k$, and
$\hat{s}(k) = \operatorname{sgn}(e(k))$ exactly. The defended closed-loop
state equation~\eqref{eq:defended_closed_loop} consequently reduces to the
unperturbed closed-loop system~\eqref{eq:closed_loop} at every step, and
the defended trajectory coincides with the unperturbed response over the
full simulation.

Fig.~\ref{fig:composite-attack-defense} presents the defended state
trajectory $x_2(t)$ overlaid with the attacked trajectory for direct
comparison (top panel), along with tracking error $e_2(t)$ for both
systems (bottom panel). The defended trace reproduces the unperturbed
reference response, while the attacked trace exhibits the degraded
tracking of the undefended system.
