\subsection{Simulation of Defended CSTR System}

The voting scheme defense is applied to the CSTR system from
\eqref{eq:cstr_dynamics} under a mathematically identical adversarial perturbation
as was used in Fig.~\ref{fig:attacked-state-tracking}. Three parallel processing units
independently compute the tracking error. Unit~3 is corrupted with noise
$\sigma = 2$, while units~1 and~2 are ideal.

Fig.~\ref{fig:defended-state-tracking} presents the defended state trajectories.
Under the voting scheme, the state variables $x_1(t)$ and $x_2(t)$ recover
nominal tracking performance, confirming the validity of
Theorem~\ref{thm:defended_convergence}.
