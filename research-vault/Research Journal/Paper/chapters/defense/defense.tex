\section{Defense Mechanisms}

The adversarial attacks discussed in this paper degrades tracking performance by
perturbing the error signal used in the sign activation function. This implies
that the computation is being attacked rather than the physical system. Therefore,
we propose a system utilizing multiple, independent, parallel processing
units. Under the condition that only 1 of the units are compromised by the attack,
a defense mechanism can be designed which almost surely recovers the exact behavior
of the nominal system, even under attack. This paper designs the implementation
of a voting scheme using $n=3$ parallel processing units is
developed and analyzed below. The remaining methods are left for future investigation.
