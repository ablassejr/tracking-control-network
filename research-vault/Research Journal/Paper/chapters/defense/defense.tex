\section{Defense Mechanisms}

The adversarial perturbation described in the preceding section degrades tracking
performance by corrupting the error signal used in the control law. A defense
mechanism must recover a sufficiently accurate error signal to preserve the
convergence properties of the nominal system. Among several candidate approaches
--- including feed-forward switching, variance-based mitigation, and control
update skipping --- a redundant sensor voting scheme is developed and analyzed below.
The remaining methods are left for future investigation.
