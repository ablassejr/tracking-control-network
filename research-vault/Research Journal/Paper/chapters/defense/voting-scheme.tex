\subsection{Voting Scheme Defense}

Consider three redundant sensor channels, each receiving the reference signal
$r(k)$. Channel $i$ receives
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:channel_reference}
    r_i(k) = r(k) + \delta_i(k) + \alpha_i \epsilon(k)
\end{equation}
where $\delta_i(k)$ is bounded sensor noise with $\|\delta_i(k)\| \leq \delta_{\max}$
for all $i$, $\epsilon(k) \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 \mathcal{I}_n)$ is the
adversarial perturbation, and $\alpha_i \in \{0, 1\}$ with
$\sum_{i=1}^{3} \alpha_i = 1$ indicates the single corrupted channel.

Each channel computes its tracking error as
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:channel_error}
    e_i(k) = x(k) - r_i(k) = e(k) - \delta_i(k) - \alpha_i \epsilon(k)
\end{equation}
where $e(k) = x(k) - r(k)$ is the true error from \autoref{eq:error_dynamics}.
Under ideal sensor conditions ($\delta_i(k) = 0$), the two clean channels
recover $e(k)$ exactly, while the corrupted channel yields
$e(k) - \epsilon(k)$.

The pairwise disagreement metric between channels $i$ and $j$ is defined as
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:pairwise_disagreement}
    V_{ij}(k) = \|e_i(k) - e_j(k)\|^2
\end{equation}

The minimum-variance pair is selected by
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:pair_selection}
    (i^*, j^*) = \underset{(i,j)}{\arg\min} \; V_{ij}(k)
\end{equation}

The defended error signal is the average of the selected pair:
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:defended_error}
    \hat{e}(k) = \frac{e_{i^*}(k) + e_{j^*}(k)}{2}
\end{equation}

Substituting $\hat{e}(k)$ into the control law from \autoref{eq:control_function}
gives the defended control input
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:defended_control}
    \hat{u}(k) = g(x(k))^{-1} \bigl( -f(x(k)) + \Delta r(k) - \beta \operatorname{sgn}(\hat{e}(k)) \bigr)
\end{equation}
and the defended closed-loop state equation
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:defended_closed_loop}
    x(k+1) = x(k) - \beta \operatorname{sgn}(\hat{e}(k)) + \Delta r(k)
\end{equation}
