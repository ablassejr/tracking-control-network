\subsubsection*{\centering Perturbed Tracking Equation}

The mathematical representation of the adversarially perturbed system can be expressed, using equation~(\ref{systemModel}) as follows:
\begin{equation}
	x(k+1) = x(k) + \mathlarger{\tau} \bigl[ f(x(k)) +  g(x(k)) u(k)                      \bigr] + I(k)
\end{equation}
where $I(k)$ represents the adversarial \textit{intrusion} at time step $k$.
