\subsection{Simulation of Attacked System}

The same CSTR plant and parameters are used. Additive Gaussian noise with
$\sigma = 2$ is injected into the signal function. The trajectories of
state variables under adversarial perturbation are presented in
Fig.~\ref{fig:attacked-state-tracking}. Both state variables exhibit
degraded tracking performance, with visible oscillations around the
reference trajectories.
