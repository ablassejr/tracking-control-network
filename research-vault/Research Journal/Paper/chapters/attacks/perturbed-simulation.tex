\subsection{Simulation of Attacked CSTR System}

The trajectories of state variables $x_1(k)$, $x_2(k)$ under adversarial
perturbation and the reference trajectories $r_1(k)$, $r_2(k)$ are presented in
Fig.~\ref{fig:attacked-state-tracking}. Both state variables exhibit degraded
tracking performance once the stochastic perturbation with a standard
deviation ($\sigma$) of 2 is introduced, with visible oscillations around the
reference trajectories throughout the simulation.

The same CSTR plant from \eqref{eq:cstr_dynamics} is used with identical
parameters. The reference trajectories are identical to
\eqref{eq:cstr_references}. The tracking error from \eqref{eq:error_dynamics}, $e(k)$,
is replaced by the perturbed error
$\tilde{e}(k)$.
