\subsection{Simulation of Attacked System}

The CSTR system from \eqref{eq:cstr_dynamics} is simulated under the
perturbed closed-loop dynamics~\eqref{eq:perturbed_closed_loop}. At each
time step, the additive disturbance $\epsilon(k)$ is drawn from a zero-mean
Gaussian source with standard deviation $\sigma = 1$ and rejection-sampled
to satisfy $|\epsilon(k)| < \tfrac{1}{2}$, so the perturbation is bounded by
the rounding-recovery threshold in every realization.

The attacked CSTR exhibits degraded tracking with visible
oscillations of $x_2(t)$ around the reference trajectory $r_2(t)$.
A direct comparison against the defended system is shown in
Fig.~\ref{fig:composite-attack-defense}.
