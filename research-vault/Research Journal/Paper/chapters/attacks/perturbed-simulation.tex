\subsection{Simulation of Attacked System}

The CSTR system from \eqref{eq:cstr_dynamics} is simulated under the
perturbed closed-loop dynamics~\eqref{eq:perturbed_closed_loop}. Additive
Gaussian noise with $\sigma = 0.5$ is injected into the output of the signal
function, introducing a random perturbation to the control signal at each
time step.

The attacked CSTR exhibits degraded tracking with visible
oscillations of $x_1(t)$ around the reference trajectory $r_1(t)$.
A direct comparison against the defended system is shown in
Fig.~\ref{fig:composite-attack-defense}.
