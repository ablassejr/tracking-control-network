\section{Adversarial Attacks}

\subsection*{
	Attacks Against a Tracking Control Neural Network
}
The general form of the discrete-time affine-in-the-inputs nonlinear system,
can be given by:
\begin{definition}
	\begin{equation}
		x(k) = F(x(k), r(k))
	\end{equation}
	where:

	\setlength{\itemsep}{0.35em}
	\begingroup \leftskip=2em
	\item[{\makebox{\boldmath$x$}:}] System state.
	\item[{\makebox{\boldmath$F$}:}] Nonlinear function that defines the system's internal state dynamics.
	\item[{\makebox{\boldmath$r$}:}] Reference trajectory that the system will attempt to follow.


	\endgroup
\end{definition}
