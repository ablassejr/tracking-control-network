against adversarial attacks is described
in~\cite{madry2019deeplearningmodelsresistant}. In this paper, we examine
an alternative method of attack modeling using additive stochastic

on the output of the signal function. This disturbance model follows the treatment of
controller nonlinearities in \cite{tymoshchuk2023nnoptimal}, where random noises
with normal distribution and zero mean are shown to affect the output of the activation
function and nonlinear dynamics of the controlled system.
