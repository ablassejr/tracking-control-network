## 📖 Definition
> **Difference Equations:** An equation that describes systems wherein the rate of change of a function, *f(x)* is dependent upon the value of *f* at *x*: $$n \epsilon \mathbb{Z} | \frac{f(x+n) - f(x)}{n} $$

---

## 🧠 Intuitive Explanation
*Explain in simple terms, as if to someone unfamiliar:* 
In a difference equation there is a recursive relationship, where the equation is only defined at certain states, and the value of the equation at a certain state is dependent upon the value of the same equation in its previous state.
