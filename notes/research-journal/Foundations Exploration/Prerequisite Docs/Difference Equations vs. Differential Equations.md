#### Differential Equations -
describe systems wherein, a function, *f(x)*, *f'(x)* is dependent upon the value, *f(x)*, and where possible states are defined continuously across their domain. 
#### Difference Equations -
Describe systems wherein the rate of change of a function, *f(x)*, where $$n \epsilon \mathbb{Z} | \frac{f(x+n) - f(x)}{n} $$  $$$$ is dependent upon the value of *f* at *x*. 
Also knows as a "Recurrence Relation"
In plain english it means: To find the next state, apply function ,*f*, to the current state.
